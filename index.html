how it works
  • grunts are plain characters. Type them.
  • brutes (whole words, in red) die on the last letter — but a miss inside one hurts 1.5×.
  • snipers aim for 3.2s, then fire for 4 HP. bombers explode after 1.6s.
  • berserkers rip 8 HP every 1.2s. shields need two hits, juggernauts three.
  • medics heal, caches pay, splitters spawn, summoners armour letters.
  • keys you miss get scarred and come back underlined, costing double. 30 clean keys repairs one.
  • Every third line of a floor is a boss; every floor cleared offers a card.
  • You get 100 HP and it never refills by itself. Combat runs till it ends.
